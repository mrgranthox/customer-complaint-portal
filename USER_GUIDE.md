# 🏦 GCB Bank Customer Complaint Tracking System (CCTS)
## Simple, Plain-English User Guide & Help Center

Welcome to the **GCB Bank Customer Complaint Tracking System (CCTS)**. This guide was written specifically for **customers, branch staff, service managers, and auditors** who want clear, straightforward instructions without any technical jargon or computer code.

---

## 📌 Quick Summary: What Does This System Do?

In simple terms, this system makes sure that **no customer complaint is ever forgotten, lost, or delayed**.

- **For Customers**: Allows you to file a dispute (like an ATM cash issue or incorrect bank charge) from your phone or computer, gives you a unique tracking number, and lets you watch the bank work on it in real time.
- **For Bank Staff**: Provides an organized digital desk where officers can investigate issues, record their findings, contact customers, and fix errors quickly.
- **For Branch Managers**: Gives a birds-eye view of all complaints across the branch, highlights any cases that are running late, and helps assign complaints to the right officers.

## 1. For Customers: Step-by-Step Guide

### How to Lodge a Complaint in Under 2 Minutes:

1. **Click "+ Lodge New Complaint"**:
   - Find the gold button on your screen.
2. **Select What Went Wrong**:
   - Choose the category that best matches your problem:
     - 🏧 **ATM / Cash Dispense Error**: If the ATM deducted money from your account but didn't give you cash.
     - 💳 **Card Charges & Unauthorized Billing**: If you see an unexpected debit or card swipe fee.
     - 📱 **Mobile App / E-Banking Glitch**: If your online transfer failed or the app is not working.
     - 💰 **Loan / Credit Facility**: If you have questions about loan repayments or interest.
     - 🏢 **Branch Teller Service**: For counter service delays or teller inquiries.
3. **Add the Details**:
   - Type a short title (e.g., *"ATM failed to dispense GHS 500 at High Street Branch"*).
   - Tell us what happened, which branch it took place at, and the date it occurred.
4. **Click "Submit Official Complaint"**:
   - The system will instantly issue you a unique reference code, such as `GCB-20261003-8821`.
   - **Keep this reference number** for your records!

---

### How to Check the Status of Your Complaint:

- **Look at Your Dashboard**:
  - All complaints you have submitted appear on your screen with their current status:
    - 🟡 **Submitted (Yellow)**: The bank received your grievance and it is queued for review.
    - 🔵 **In Progress (Blue)**: An investigation officer is actively working on your case.
    - 🟢 **Resolved (Green)**: The issue has been fixed and a full explanation has been provided.
- **Click "View Timeline"**:
  - Click on any complaint to read notes from the investigating officer, see the root cause, and check when action was taken.
- **Track by Reference Number**:
  - If you only have your tracking code, enter it in the **"Track Dispute"** search box to see immediate updates.

## 2. For Bank Staff & Investigation Officers

As an Investigation Officer, your job is to review, troubleshoot, and resolve customer grievances within the target time limit.

### Daily Routine:

1. **Check "My Assigned Queue"**:
   - This screen lists all cases assigned directly to you.
   - Look at the **SLA Countdown Timer**:
     - 🟢 **Green Timer**: Plenty of time remaining.
     - 🟠 **Amber Timer**: Less than 12 hours remaining — prioritize this case!
     - 🔴 **Red Timer**: Overdue / Breached — immediate action required.
2. **Open the Case File**:
   - Click **"Investigate Case"** to open the full customer dossier.
   - Read the customer's explanation, account number, and incident date.
3. **Conduct Your Investigation**:
   - Check the core banking logs (e.g. ATM electronic journal, switch network logs, or reversal slips).
4. **Log Progress Notes**:
   - Write an update note (e.g., *"Checked ATM journal tape; confirmed cash purge of GHS 500. Initiating ledger credit reversal."*).
   - This note is saved permanently with your name and exact timestamp.
5. **Mark the Case as Resolved**:
   - When the issue is corrected (e.g. funds refunded or service restored), enter the **Root Cause** and the **Final Resolution Note**.
   - Click **"Mark as Resolved & Close Case"**. The customer will immediately see that their dispute is resolved!

## 4. The 3 Simple Steps of Every Complaint

Every dispute in the bank moves through three transparent stages:

```
  [ 1. SUBMITTED ]              [ 2. IN PROGRESS ]              [ 3. RESOLVED ]
 
 Customer files report      Officer is assigned to it      Issue is fixed & confirmed
 Reference code created     Logs checks & calling notes    Customer receives refund/fix
 Timer starts counting      Customer can see active work    Case is closed permanently
```

- **Target Times (SLAs)**:
  - 🚨 **Urgent** (e.g. Fraud, duplicate unauthorized charges): **24 Hours**
  - ⚠️ **High** (e.g. ATM failed cash dispense): **48 Hours**
  - ℹ️ **Medium** (e.g. Mobile app login troubles): **72 Hours**
  - 📝 **Low** (e.g. Statement format suggestions): **120 Hours (5 Days)**

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Can other customers see my personal complaints or account details?
**Answer**: **No, never.** The system has strict digital privacy barriers. Each customer can only see their own submitted cases. Even if someone guesses your complaint ID, the bank system automatically blocks them from opening it.

### Q: What should I do if an ATM kept my card or didn't give me cash?
**Answer**:
1. Lodge a complaint under **"ATM / Cash Dispense Error"**.
2. Include the ATM location (e.g. *Airport Branch ATM 2*) and the approximate time.
3. Keep your reference code. Officers check the ATM electronic audit roll and process refunds directly into your account.

### Q: How do I know when my issue has been solved?
**Answer**:
When you log in, your complaint badge will turn green (**"Resolved"**). You can open it to read the officer's exact explanation and see the resolution timestamp.

### Q: What if an officer takes too long to respond?
**Answer**:
The system tracks the clock continuously. If an officer does not resolve the issue within the target hours, the case turns **Red (Breached)** on the Branch Manager's dashboard so the manager can intervene immediately.

## 6. Plain-English Glossary (Jargon Buster)

| Term | What It Means in Plain English |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CCTS** | *Customer Complaint Tracking System* — the official name of this web platform. |
| **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** | A strict promise made by the bank on how many hours we have to fix a problem before it is considered late. |
| **Triage** | The process of quickly reviewing a new complaint to decide who is the best person to fix it. |
| **Reference Number** | A unique code (like `GCB-20261003-8821`) given to every complaint so anyone can find it in seconds. |
| **Audit Trail** | An automatic digital history book that records who touched the case, what notes they wrote, and what time they did it. Nobody can alter or delete this history. |
| **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)** | A security rule that makes sure customers only see customer screens, staff only see staff screens, and managers have supervision tools. |
| **IDOR Protection** | A security guard built into the software that stops unauthorized people from peeking at your personal bank records. |
